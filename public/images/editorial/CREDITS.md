These locally stored photos are topic illustrations, not photographs of the reported event. Source-supplied article photographs always take precedence. Each fallback is visibly labelled “Illustrative photo.” No generated imagery or random-image endpoint is used.

Location and event details must not be inferred from illustrative photos. Unclassified stories use the reporting photograph; expanding this collection and keyword mappings improves specificity for new subjects. Photographs are presentation fallbacks only: article records, publication, source text, and dates are untouched.
